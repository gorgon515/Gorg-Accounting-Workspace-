Practice

What we actually do.

Four disciplines, advised by the partners who are accountable for them. We say what we do, and we do only that.

01
Cross-Border Structuring
Holding structures, treaty positions, transfer pricing, and entity selection. We design for the audit that may never come — and would survive it if it did.
02
Transaction Advisory
We model tax consequences before a deal is signed: acquisitions, carve-outs, dispositions, and internal reorganizations across jurisdictions.
03
Controversy & Audit
Representation before the IRS and foreign tax authorities. Positions are documented to withstand scrutiny rather than to invite negotiation.
04
Private Capital & Succession
Structures for families, founders, and closely held capital — built for the decades that follow, not the quarter ahead.
