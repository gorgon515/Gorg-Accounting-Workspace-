Practice

What we actually do.

Four disciplines, advised by the partners who are accountable for them. We say what we do, and we do only that.

01 · Cross-Border Structuring

Built to hold across borders.

Holding structures, treaty positions, transfer pricing, and entity selection. We design for the audit that may never come — and would survive it if it did.

  • Holding-company and IP-holding design
  • Treaty access and beneficial-ownership analysis
  • Transfer-pricing policy and documentation
  • Entity selection and check-the-box planning

02 · Transaction Advisory

Modeled before it is signed.

We model tax consequences before a deal closes: acquisitions, carve-outs, dispositions, and internal reorganizations across jurisdictions. We are least useful once the structure is set.

i

Buy-side & sell-side

Structuring the acquisition or disposition before the term sheet, not after the close.

ii

Carve-outs & step plans

Sequencing the steps so each one stands on its own and the whole holds together.

iii

Earn-outs & rollovers

The taxation of contingent and rolled consideration, modeled before it is agreed.

iv

Post-close reorganization

Integration and internal restructuring once the transaction has closed.

03 · Controversy & Audit

Documented to withstand scrutiny.

i

Examination

Representation before the IRS and foreign authorities, from first notice to resolution.

ii

Voluntary disclosure

Coming into compliance on prior positions, quietly and on defensible terms.

iii

Appeals

Positions are documented to withstand scrutiny rather than to invite negotiation.

04 · Private Capital & Succession

For the decades that follow.

i

Succession

Structures for families, founders, and closely held capital — built for the decade after the founder.

ii

Trust & estate

Cross-border estate planning that assumes more than one governing law.

iii

Governance

The structure documented so it can be administered without the founder in the room.
